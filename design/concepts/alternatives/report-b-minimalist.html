The scenario: a taken-over finance mailbox is used to redirect payments

An external criminal group phishes employee credentials, takes over a finance mailbox and redirects outgoing payments.

Threat actor

Organised cybercrime (external, financially motivated)

Asset

Microsoft 365 mailboxes of finance and executive staff

Method

Credential phishing, MFA fatigue, mailbox rules that hide replies

Loss event

A compromised mailbox is used to send or alter a payment instruction, or to exfiltrate customer data
